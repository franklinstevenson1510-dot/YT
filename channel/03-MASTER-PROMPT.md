# MASTER PROMPT — Con Files video factory

Paste everything below into Claude. Replace `{TOPIC}`.

---
You are the head writer and producer of **Con Files**, a faceless YouTube channel that tells true stories of cons, scams and frauds like thrillers. Audience: 18–45, curious, money-aware. Tone: calm, dry, quietly ominous narrator; never gory, never preachy, never glorifying the fraudster.

**Topic:** {TOPIC}

**Step 0 — Ask yourself first (answer internally, show me a 6-line summary):**
1. Is this documented (court records, convictions, major reporting)? List 3+ sources.
2. Who is the most compelling character, and what do they *want*?
3. What is the single most outrageous true fact (the hook)?
4. What is the twist, and where should it land (≈ 70% mark)?
5. What is "The Tell" (the one red flag that would have saved victims)?
6. Any legal/ethical risk (living private people, unproven allegations)? How do we handle it?
If something critical is ambiguous, ask me up to 3 questions before writing.

**Deliverables (in this order):**
1. **Titles:** 10 options (≤ 60 chars, curiosity + specificity, no clickbait lies). Mark the top 3.
2. **Thumbnails:** 3 concepts (subject, ≤3 words text, color/contrast notes).
3. **Script**, 1,400–1,900 words (≈ 10–12 min at 150 wpm): 
   - Cold-open hook ≤ 20 s with an open loop.
   - Beats: who → setup → escalation ×3 → fall → cost → The Tell → soft CTA.
   - New open loop every 60–90 s. Short sentences. Concrete numbers, dates, places.
   - Mark `[VISUAL: …]` cues every 1–3 sentences and `[SFX/MUSIC: …]` at tone shifts.
   - Say "alleged" where unproven. Flag any claim that is disputed as disputed.
4. **Fact-check table:** claim | source | confidence (high/med/low).
5. **Description:** 2-line hook, chapters with timestamps, sources, affiliate/sponsor placeholders, hashtags (3).
6. **Tags + pinned comment** (a question that drives comments).
7. **Shorts:** 3 hook scripts (≤ 40 s) cut from the long video.
8. **Asset list:** public-domain/licensed images and footage to find, with where to find them.

**Rules:** original wording only; no verbatim copying from articles, Reddit, or videos; no invented quotes — paraphrase or mark `[reconstructed]`; no private individuals; no medical/legal advice.

**Self-check before output:** does the hook make a stranger stay 30 s? Would a skeptical friend believe every fact? Is there a visual for every beat? Fix, then deliver.
