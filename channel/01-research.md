# Research

## Snook (reference channel)
- Faceless narrator. Content: horror/Reddit stories, iceberg explainers, "unexplainable" and unsettling themes, plus a podcast (M/W/F).
- ~640K subs / 75M+ views as of Jan 2026 (per Grokipedia/Social Blade summaries; verify live before quoting).
- Why it works (the transferable parts): 1) one calm narrator voice with a strong tone, 2) dread + curiosity hooks, 3) iceberg/"layers" structure that rewards watching to the end, 4) high volume, repeatable template, 5) podcast repurposing.

## Where Snook's niche under-monetizes
- Horror/disturbing content is often limited-ads or demonetized; brand sponsors avoid it. RPM is typically low.
- Reddit/user-submitted stories carry rights + reuse risk, and YouTube's "inauthentic/mass-produced content" rules target templated reused material.

## Market data (secondary sources, treat as ranges)
- Finance/investing: ~$15–45 CPM. Faceless storytelling: ~$15–25. Cybersecurity explainers: ~$20–30.
- Long-form business docs (MagnatesMedia, ColdFusion, Company Man style) are among the best faceless formats: 10+ min watch time, premium advertisers, strong sponsors.

## Niche decision: **Cons, scams & frauds — told like a thriller**
Intersection of:
- **Entertainment:** twists, charismatic villains, "how did anyone fall for this", built-in cliffhangers. Snook-style dread/curiosity pacing transfers directly.
- **Profitability:** money/fraud topics attract finance-adjacent advertisers (higher CPM) and sponsors (VPN, identity protection, password managers, banks/fintech).
- **Advertiser-friendly:** no gore; fraud is not "sensitive events" the way violent crime is.
- **Evergreen + endless supply:** historical cons (public domain), famous frauds, modern scams, "iceberg of scams".
- **Spin-offs:** shorts, podcast, iceberg series ("The Scam Iceberg"), "scam of the week" for current events.

Rejected: pure horror (low RPM), pure finance explainers (crowded, dry), true crime/violent (limited ads, high competition).

## Risk rules
- Only cover cases with court records, convictions, settlements, or major reporting. Say "alleged" until convicted. No private individuals.
- Original script + original/licensed visuals only (YouTube inauthentic-content policy). No reading others' posts verbatim.
- Disclose AI voice/imagery where YouTube requires altered/synthetic disclosure for realistic content.
