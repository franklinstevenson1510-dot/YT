# Channel Blueprint

## Name options (check handle availability yourself)
1. **Con Files** (recommended — short, searchable, series-able)
2. The Last Mark
3. Hollow Pockets
4. Paper Tigers
5. Marked

## Positioning
"True stories of the smartest liars alive — and the people who believed them." Tone: calm, dry, slightly ominous narrator; cinematic and clean. Not edgy.

## Bio / About (paste)
Every week, one con, one scam, one liar — told like a thriller. Cases are drawn from court records and major reporting. New videos weekly. Business: <your email>

## Visual identity
- Palette: near-black `#0B0B0F`, paper `#EDE7DA`, accent signal red `#D7263D`.
- Fonts: Bebas Neue (titles), Inter (body captions).
- Thumbnail grammar: ONE subject (archival face/object, high contrast, grayscale + red accent), 3 words max, a visual "tell" (red circle/arrow/redaction bar).
- Banner: "TRUE STORIES OF THE SMARTEST LIARS" + upload schedule.

## Format rules
- Length 9–13 min (mid-rolls at natural beats, ~8+ min enables them).
- Hook in first 20 s: the most outrageous fact + open loop. No intro logo.
- Structure: Cold open → "who is this" → the setup → escalation (3 beats) → the twist/fall → what it cost → the lesson ("the tell").
- Retention: a new open loop every ~60–90 s; visual change every 3–6 s.
- Recurring segment: **"The Tell"** — the one red flag that would have saved the victims. Brand + sponsor slot for protection products.

## Production stack (cheap)
- Script: Claude (using `03-MASTER-PROMPT.md`), human fact-check pass.
- Voice: ElevenLabs/similar AI voice or your own voice; keep it consistent.
- Visuals: public-domain archives (Library of Congress, Wikimedia Commons, Internet Archive, NARA), licensed stock, simple motion graphics (text, maps, redaction bars).
- Edit: CapCut / DaVinci Resolve (free). Captions on.
- Thumbnail: Canva/Photoshop.

## 30-day plan (weekly, 4 long videos + 8 shorts)
1. **The Man Who Sold the Eiffel Tower** (Victor Lustig) — video-001 (packaged)
2. The Fake Prince Who Fooled Wall Street (Gerald Barnbaum / "Prince" cons) — verify case
3. The Con That Fooled the Art World (Han van Meegeren / forged Vermeers)
4. The Scam Iceberg (Tier 1 → Tier 5) — Snook-style iceberg format for the algorithm
Shorts: 20–40 s hook cut of each long video + "The Tell" clips.

## Monetization path
1. Weeks 1–12: ship ≥ 12 videos, iterate on CTR/retention.
2. YPP: 1,000 subs + 4,000 public watch hours (or 10M Shorts views/90 days) for ads. Fan-funding unlocks at lower thresholds (500 subs + 3 uploads) — confirm current terms in YouTube Studio.
3. Sponsors from ~5–10K subs: VPN, identity theft protection, password managers, fintech.
4. Affiliate: books about each case (Amazon Associates), scam-protection tools.
5. Later: podcast version, newsletter, "Scam of the Week" community posts.

## KPIs
CTR ≥ 5%, avg view duration ≥ 40%, 30-day: 1 video >10K views is the signal to double down on that sub-topic.
